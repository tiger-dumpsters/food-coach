Getting started

How do I join?

HungerHush is in private beta. Open the invite link a friend sent you and sign in with Google. If you have an invite or household code instead, sign in at gethungerhush.com/app and enter it when asked.

How do I put it on my home screen?

iPhone: open gethungerhush.com/app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

What do I tell the coach?

Whatever you'd text a friend: "two eggs and toast," "hungry again and it's only 10:30," or "walked 30 minutes." You can also send a photo of your plate or a nutrition label (Plus). Tell it when you're hungry even if you don't eat; that's how it learns your patterns.

How accurate are the numbers?

They're estimates, built to be consistent so your patterns hold up. Amounts, brands, and how something was cooked all help. Tap "Breakdown" on any meal to see what the coach assumed, and tell it if something's off.

Fixing things

The coach logged something wrong

Just tell it: "that was yesterday," "it was half a sandwich," or "delete the cookies." If a reply seems off, tap Flag under it and say what went wrong. Flags come straight to us.

I got "Lost the connection before that went through"

Your message is still in the box. Check your connection and tap send again. On some phones, switching apps while the coach is replying can interrupt it.

I'm not getting notifications

Turn them on in the You tab. On iPhone, notifications only work when HungerHush is added to your home screen (iOS 16.4 or later) and allowed in Settings → Notifications → HungerHush.

I can't sign in

Make sure you're using the same Google account you joined with. If you see "invite-only," open your invite link again or ask the person who invited you for a new one.

Households

How do households work?

Link with a partner or family to share a pantry list and log meals you ate together for everyone at once ("Alexa and I both had tacos"). Your chat, hunger notes, targets, and weight stay private. Starting a household and inviting people is part of Plus; joining someone's household is free.

How do I leave a household?

You tab → Household → Leave household. Your own log stays with you.

Plans and billing

What's the difference between Free and Plus?

Free covers chat logging, hunger patterns, and trends, with a daily message limit. Plus adds photo logging, households, deeper answers, and a higher daily limit.

How do I upgrade, change my card, or cancel?

You tab → Your plan. Upgrade opens a secure Stripe checkout. Once you're on Plus, Manage subscription lets you update your card, see receipts, or cancel. If you cancel, you keep Plus until the end of the month you paid for.

Your data

Who can see my log?

Other users can't. Household members see only the shared pantry and meals you log for each other. See our privacy policy for the full details.

How do I export my log?

You tab → Your data → Export log (CSV). It opens in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.

How do I delete my account?

You tab → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. This permanently deletes your log, chat, photos, and settings, removes you from any household, and cancels any Plus subscription. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Still need help?

Email hello@gethungerhush.com and tell us what's going on. A screenshot helps. We'll get back to you within a few days.
