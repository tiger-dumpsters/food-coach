Two words we built it on

Hush.

We know what it feels like to be thinking about lunch before finishing your breakfast. Obsessing about your next sweet treat. Regretting your last food choice.

Turn down the food noise. Understand when and why hunger gets loud, so it stops running your day.

Nourish.

Real food you enjoy, keeping you full and satisfied. Eat when you’re hungry, without the guilt, and learn which meals actually carry you.

Our chat will take away the stress of deciding what and when to eat. No diets, no scolding, and absolutely no ignoring hunger cues.

How it works

No food database to search, no barcodes, no forms. Just talk to our coach.

  1. Text what you eat

    Plain words, a photo of your plate, or a nutrition label. The coach figures out the rest.

    “two eggs and toast, coffee with oat milk”

  2. Tell us when you’re hungry

    Even when you don’t eat. Even when you eat something that brings up feelings of guilt or shame. Even when you think you shouldn’t be hungry. These moments are how HungerHush learns your rhythm.

    “hungry again, it’s only 10:30”

  3. See what keeps you full

    Patterns show when hunger hits, your toughest moments, and which meals hold you longest.

    “the yogurt bowl holds you about 3 hours”

Made for the always-hungry

Living with food noise

Constant thoughts about food, grazing that feels constant, and hunger that comes back too fast. Hunger that simply won’t quit.

On, off, or considering a GLP-1

Nobody talks about the conversations you’re having before starting a GLP-1 or when you stop taking it. How can we make hunger sustainable? HungerHush helps build habits that keep you satisfied and works with your rebound hunger and extra-loud food noise. No going hungry here!

Cooking for a family

Meals that work for everyone at the table, plus ideas from what’s already in your kitchen. We learn your pantry staples, dietary restrictions, and family preferences to help make suggestions realistic and convenient.

Tired of dieting

No points, no forbidden foods. Weight tracking is there if you want it and hidden if you don’t. This is about nourishment and finding freedom from food noise, not ignoring hunger or following a restrictive diet.
